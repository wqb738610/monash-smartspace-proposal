# Monash SmartSpace - AI/IoT Campus Space Optimization Proposal

Monash SmartSpace is a project-management and business-analysis proposal for an AI and IoT powered campus study-space optimization system. The concept aims to help students find available study spaces and understand space type, crowding, and accessibility information.

## Project Type

Business justification, project scope definition, AI/IoT concept design, project management.

## My Contribution

- Prepared and refined the business justification for an AI/IoT campus study-space system.
- Defined the 12-month pilot scope, key deliverables, success criteria, assumptions, and out-of-scope boundaries.
- Translated student pain points into platform requirements around occupancy visibility, study preferences, and accessibility.
- Considered privacy, accessibility, and ethical constraints, including anonymous occupancy sensing and avoiding facial recognition.
